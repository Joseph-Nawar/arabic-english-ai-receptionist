# Canonical roadmap

This is the approved 13-phase roadmap for the Arabic-English AI Receptionist & Customer Operations Agent portfolio project. The project is a production-style portfolio implementation, not a SaaS startup. Phase 12 is the fixed finish line.

| Phase | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Engineering Foundation & Architecture | `FIX REQUIRED` |
| 1 | Core Domain Model & Business Configuration | `NOT STARTED` |
| 2 | Identity, Service Area & Operating Rules | `NOT STARTED` |
| 3 | Conversations, Turns & Audit Trail | `NOT STARTED` |
| 4 | Booking Workflow & Handoff Rules | `NOT STARTED` |
| 5 | Calendar Integration | `NOT STARTED` |
| 6 | CRM Integration | `NOT STARTED` |
| 7 | Messaging and WhatsApp Channel | `NOT STARTED` |
| 8 | Voice and Voice-Note Channel | `NOT STARTED` |
| 9 | LLM Behavior, Tools & Knowledge Retrieval | `NOT STARTED` |
| 10 | Operator UI | `NOT STARTED` |
| 11 | Asynchronous Operations, Hardening & Observability | `NOT STARTED` |
| 12 | Final Demo, Review & Finish Line | `NOT STARTED` |

Phase 0 ends this implementation as `REVIEW`. It is not `COMPLETE`; independent human review and approval are required. Phases 1–12 remain `NOT STARTED`, and no Phase 1 behavior is included in this branch.
